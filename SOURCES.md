## Cultural Sensitivity Note

When researching bakeneko and other yokai, please remember:
- These are living cultural traditions with deep roots in Japanese culture
- Respect for cultural context is essential
- Indigenous/traditional knowledge should be approached respectfully
- Popular media interpretations may differ from traditional folklore
